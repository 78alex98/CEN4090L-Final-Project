## 2) Project Title and Description
Title: Bartering

Description: Enables users to create accounts, list items for trade, make bids with their registered items, and build reputation through a rating and review system.


## 3) Accomplishments and overall project status during this increment
The final increment of the project did not go as planned due to the tragic event of April 17 at Florida State University. Following this, members of the group have made their decisions regarding project completion according to the university's guidelines as well as the guidelines and options presented for finishing the course project.

That said, while we were not able to implement all the functionality and behaviour that we had planned, we have at least completed a minimum version of the project that includes working variants of most of the planned features. For increment 3, accomplishments includes basic support for uploading and persisting item images, fetching and viewing a user's inventory, listing items, placing bids on items, and functionality for the owner of the listed item to select a winning bid.

## 4) Challenges, changes in the plan and scope of the project and things that went wrong during this increment
**Refer to section 3 above.**

A major change in the scope/plan that occurred is our decision to have the endpoint for selecting a winning bid to delete the listing and swap the ownership of the listed item and the winning item, effectively simulating a trade between two users. We made this decision purely due to time constraints. Our original plan was to have some sort of messaging system where the winner would get a message that may then include contact information. The existence of the "message" attribute of a listing was part of this plan, where it might have served as an automated message to be sent to the winner.
